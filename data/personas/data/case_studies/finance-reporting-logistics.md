# Monthly finance reporting automation for a logistics firm

## Context
Mosaic Logistics needed automated board reports in Power BI. The existing process was slow and error-prone.

## What I built
I designed and delivered automated board reports in Power BI. The stack: pivot tables and lookup formulas for the finance team, Microsoft dashboards with measures written in a formula language, SQL, data pipeline, a serverless warehouse on Google Cloud billed per query.

## Outcome
The result close process 8 days shorter.
