# Checkout A/B test analysis for an real estate retailer

## Context
Northwind Real Estate needed a rigorous readout of a pricing-page experiment. The existing process was slow and error-prone.

## What I built
I designed and delivered a rigorous readout of a pricing-page experiment. The stack: hypothesis testing, scripting the whole backend end to end, Pandas, window functions and CTEs over the orders tables.

## Outcome
The result identified a 54% conversion lift.
