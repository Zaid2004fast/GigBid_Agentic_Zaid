# Marketing funnel and attribution analysis (e-commerce)

## Context
Pioneer E-Commerce needed a funnel model from GA4 exports in BigQuery. The existing process was slow and error-prone.

## What I built
I designed and delivered a funnel model from GA4 exports in BigQuery. The stack: GA4, a serverless warehouse on Google Cloud billed per query, SQL, python3.

## Outcome
The result reallocated 25% of ad spend.
