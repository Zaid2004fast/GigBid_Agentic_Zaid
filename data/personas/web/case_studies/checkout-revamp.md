# Checkout and subscription billing for an real estate store

## Context
Kestrel Real Estate needed a new checkout with subscriptions and dunning emails. The existing process was slow and error-prone.

## What I built
I designed and delivered a new checkout with subscriptions and dunning emails. The stack: a component-based single-page front end with hooks, subscription billing with webhooks for failed payments, Node.js, REST routes with middleware for auth and validation, a relational database with migrations and indexes tuned for slow queries, background workers that process uploads off the request path.

## Outcome
The result checkout conversion up 67%.
