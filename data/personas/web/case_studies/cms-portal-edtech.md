# Content portal with admin panel for a edtech publisher

## Context
Corvid Edtech needed an editorial CMS with custom workflows. The existing process was slow and error-prone.

## What I built
I designed and delivered an editorial CMS with custom workflows. The stack: Django REST Framework, Postgres, a component-based single-page front end with hooks, Tailwind CSS, an in-memory cache in front of the database.

## Outcome
The result editors publish 58% faster.
