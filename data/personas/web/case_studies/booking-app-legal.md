# Booking and scheduling web app for a legal clinic network

## Context
Evergreen Legal needed online booking with reminders and deposits. The existing process was slow and error-prone.

## What I built
I designed and delivered online booking with reminders and deposits. The stack: NextJS, strictly typed front-end and API contracts, a relational database with migrations and indexes tuned for slow queries, utility-first styling with a design-token config, Stripe, scripting the whole backend end to end.
