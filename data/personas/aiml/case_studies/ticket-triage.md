# Support ticket triage with a fine-tuned language model (media)

## Context
Harbor Media needed a fine-tuned classifier that routes tickets to the right queue. The existing process was slow and error-prone.

## What I built
I designed and delivered a fine-tuned classifier that routes tickets to the right queue. The stack: fine-tuned a pretrained encoder from the model hub, QLoRA, torch, classified free-text support tickets by intent, scripting the whole backend end to end, Qdrant.

## Outcome
The result routing accuracy of 83% on 800 tickets.
