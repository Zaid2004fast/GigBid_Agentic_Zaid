# Weekly demand forecasting for a manufacturing distributor

## Context
Tessellate Manufacturing needed store-level demand forecasts with holiday effects. The existing process was slow and error-prone.

## What I built
I designed and delivered store-level demand forecasts with holiday effects. The stack: time series forecasting, scripting the whole backend end to end, cleaned and reshaped messy spreadsheets with dataframes, MLflow.

## Outcome
The result forecast error (MAPE) reduced to 78%.
