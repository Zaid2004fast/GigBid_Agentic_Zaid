# Internal knowledge assistant for a edtech company

## Context
Corvid Edtech needed a chat assistant that answers staff questions from 1200 internal documents. The existing process was slow and error-prone.

## What I built
I designed and delivered a chat assistant that answers staff questions from 1200 internal documents. The stack: scripting the whole backend end to end, LangChain, retrieval-augmented generation, Qdrant, FastAPI, OpenAI API, docker-compose.

## Outcome
The result cut average search time from 7 minutes to 16 seconds.
