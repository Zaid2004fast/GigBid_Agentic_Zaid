# Invoice data extraction pipeline for a e-commerce firm

## Context
Pioneer E-Commerce needed an OCR and layout model that extracts line items from scanned invoices. The existing process was slow and error-prone.

## What I built
I designed and delivered an OCR and layout model that extracts line items from scanned invoices. The stack: Python, OpenCV, torch, FastAPI, Docker, classified free-text support tickets by intent.

## Outcome
The result reduced manual entry by 66% across 800 invoices a month.
