# Real-time shelf monitoring with computer vision for media retail

## Context
Juniper Media needed a detector that flags empty shelves from store cameras. The existing process was slow and error-prone.

## What I built
I designed and delivered a detector that flags empty shelves from store cameras. The stack: YOLOv8, image preprocessing: deskewing, thresholding and contour detection, torch, docker-compose.

## Outcome
The result 71% detection mAP at 8 frames per second.
