# Customer churn prediction for a fintech subscription business

## Context
Tessellate Fintech needed a churn model with weekly scoring and retention playbooks. The existing process was slow and error-prone.

## What I built
I designed and delivered a churn model with weekly scoring and retention playbooks. The stack: python3, sklearn, pandas, MLflow, window functions and CTEs over the orders tables.

## Outcome
The result raised retention-campaign precision to 40%.
