# Semantic product search for a legal marketplace

## Context
Pioneer Legal needed embedding-based search over 20000 products. The existing process was slow and error-prone.

## What I built
I designed and delivered embedding-based search over 20000 products. The stack: python3, approximate nearest-neighbour search over millions of embeddings in memory, transformers, FastAPI, text classification.

## Outcome
The result search click-through up 44%.
