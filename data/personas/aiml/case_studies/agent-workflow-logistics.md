# Multi-agent document review workflow for a logistics team

## Context
Juniper Logistics needed agents that review contracts and flag risky clauses with human approval. The existing process was slow and error-prone.

## What I built
I designed and delivered agents that review contracts and flag risky clauses with human approval. The stack: LangGraph, python3, GPT-4, FastAPI, retrieval-augmented generation, Pandas.

## Outcome
The result review time down 87%.
