# Customer churn prediction for a edtech subscription business

## Context
Harbor Edtech needed a churn model with weekly scoring and retention playbooks. The existing process was slow and error-prone.

## What I built
I designed and delivered a churn model with weekly scoring and retention playbooks. The stack: python3, sklearn, pandas, experiment tracking and a model registry for reproducible training runs, SQL.

## Outcome
The result raised retention-campaign precision to 34%.
