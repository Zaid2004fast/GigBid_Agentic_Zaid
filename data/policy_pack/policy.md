# GigSim Marketplace Policy Pack (v2 - updated 2026-11-02)

> Stand-in written for this project because the instructor's policy pack was not distributed.
> It mirrors the four themes named in the brief. The machine-readable form the Compliance
> Guard enforces is `rules.yaml` in this folder.

## P-1 No off-platform contact or payment
Proposals and messages must not contain email addresses, phone numbers, messaging handles
(WhatsApp, Telegram, Skype…) or requests to pay outside the platform. Clients asking for this
are a scam signal.

## P-2 No fabricated experience
Every statement about past work must be true and verifiable from the freelancer's portfolio.
Inflating years of experience, inventing clients or claiming tools never used is prohibited.

## P-3 No guarantees
Freelancers must not guarantee outcomes ("guaranteed results", "100% success", "risk-free",
"money-back"). Describe evidence and approach instead.

## P-4 Fair use of automation
Automation may help draft, but a human must review and approve every proposal and message.
Bulk/spam proposals, scraping and unapproved bots are forbidden. Read calls must respect
rate limits.

## P-5 Respectful, relevant content
No unrelated links, no keyword stuffing, no copying the job post back to the client.

## P-6 Free work
Freelancers should not perform substantial unpaid work as part of a proposal. Short
screening answers are fine; complete deliverables are not.

## P-7 Screening questions (added 2 Nov 2026)
Every screening question must be answered. Connects cost is now tiered by job budget (<= $500: 4, <= $2,000: 8, above: 12).
